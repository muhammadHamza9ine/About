About the game

Word Matching Card Puzzle combines letter cards, word completion, and level-based play in one simple puzzle experience. Each challenge invites you to pay attention, remember what you have seen, and select letters in the required order before time runs out.

Play at your own pace, improve your approach with every round, and enjoy the satisfaction of completing each word.

How to play

Follow these simple steps to take on a word challenge.

Read the target word

Look at the word shown for the current puzzle and notice its letters.

Reveal and select cards

Tap the letter cards and choose the next required letter in the sequence.

Complete the puzzle

Finish the word before time or moves run out, then continue to the next challenge when available.

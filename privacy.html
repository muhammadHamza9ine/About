Privacy Policy

Word Matching Card Puzzle

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Matching Card Puzzle stores gameplay information and how Google AdMob may process information to provide advertisements.

1. Introduction

Word Matching Card Puzzle Game is an Android puzzle game. We respect users' privacy and aim to explain our data practices clearly. This policy applies to the App and its use of Google AdMob advertising services.

The App does not require users to create an account. The App uses local device storage for gameplay progress and does not use Firebase or a developer-operated online database to store game progress. The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Current level and game progress;
  • In-game coins and game settings;
  • Saved cards and current game state;
  • Timer, moves, and gameplay preferences.

This information is stored using Android app storage, such as SharedPreferences. Under the App's current design, this gameplay information is not uploaded to a developer-operated game server. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may delete saved progress.

3. Google AdMob and Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to show banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or share certain information with Google and its service providers. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, and features used, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers, where applicable;
  • Interactions with the App and advertisements, such as ad views, taps, and video views;
  • Technical, diagnostic, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure ad performance, help prevent fraud, maintain security, and provide related advertising services. Whether advertisements are personalized may depend on device settings, consent choices, location, and applicable requirements. The developer does not control Google's independent data practices.

4. How Information Is Used

Information handled by the App or its advertising provider may be used to:

  • Save and restore gameplay progress on your device;
  • Maintain game features and settings;
  • Display and measure advertisements;
  • Diagnose technical problems and assess performance;
  • Help detect fraud and protect advertising services.

5. Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell personal information collected directly by the developer. Gameplay progress stored locally is not uploaded to a developer-operated game database under the App's current design.

Google AdMob and its service providers may collect, process, or share information as needed to provide advertising, measure performance, prevent fraud, and support related services, subject to their policies and applicable law.

6. Security, Retention, and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay information remains on your device until it is deleted by you or removed through app or device data-management processes. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App may remove this information.

Advertising-related information is handled under Google's applicable security and retention practices. Google provides information about its services and privacy practices in its own policies. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The App's intended age group must be declared accurately in Google Play Console. If the App targets children or includes children in its target audience, additional Google Play Families requirements and applicable laws may apply. The developer must configure advertising services and any required consent mechanisms accordingly.

You can stop using the App or uninstall it at any time. Clearing the App's storage may delete locally saved gameplay progress.
